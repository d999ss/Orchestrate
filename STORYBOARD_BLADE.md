# Concept 3 · The Blade

**Project:** Orchestrate 2026 — GridOS Customer Conference
**Tagline:** The world runs on what we cast.
**Format:** 16:9 · ~60 sec · 27 frames (9 beats × 3 sub-shots)

## The story

The world is in an AI race. Compute is scaling exponentially. But there is a ceiling. The ceiling is not algorithms. It is not chips. It is energy. Energy means turbines. Turbines depend on one part above all others — the blade. Cast in nickel super-alloy with single-crystal precision and a thermal-barrier coating that survives temperatures hotter than the surface of the sun. There is no harder part to make. There is essentially one company that makes it at scale. The film is a quiet 60-second proof: the bottleneck of the AI era is the blade, and we are the blade.

We open on the AI race, hit the ceiling, name the constraint, hold on the object, watch it cast in fire and crystal, install it, ignite the world it powers, and end at the room in Atlanta where the audience is sitting. The Mercedes-Benz Stadium dome marks the city. The Orchestrate stage names the moment. The brand mark closes the loop.

## Visual treatment

100% dot-matrix particle aesthetic per the GE Vernova cardinal rule. Cyan-teal (#00B2A9) is the dominant particle color; lime accent (#A6FF00) is reserved for two things: heat (molten metal, plasma, ignition) and the GE Vernova brand reveal. No tablets. No computer screens. No UI. No text on glass. The film is physical objects, scale, fire, and silence — never an interface. The blade is the hero, held longer than feels comfortable.

## Production notes

- **Cold open.** 1A — the blinking cursor on pure black. Hold for a beat longer than feels right. The whole 60-second film begins from this stillness. The keypress is implied; we never show it.
- **Hero shot.** 4A — the single blade, alone, slowly rotating. This frame is the film. Hold it longer than feels right. Render order: 4A first to lock the look, then 9A (Atlanta dome) and 5A (molten pour), then iterate the rest.
- **Lime discipline.** Lime accent is reserved for HEAT (molten 5A, plasma 6B, ignition 8B) and the BRAND (9C). Nothing else gets lime. Cyan does all other work.
- **No UI.** No tablets. No screens with text. No charts. No data visualizations on glass. If a beat reads as "operator looking at a display," redesign it as a physical action. The film is mass, fire, and silence.
- **Atlanta presence.** Beat 07 and Beat 09 must read unmistakably as Atlanta. Mercedes-Benz Stadium dome is the iconic city marker — the ETFE petal roof is the silhouette to chase. Georgia World Congress Center adjacency implied for the Orchestrate stage.
- **Faces.** No identifiable faces anywhere. Silhouettes only at 7A.
- **Endcard.** 9C is the last frame. The brand mark is in lime; everything else stays cyan/black.
