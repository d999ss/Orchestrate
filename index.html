Concept 04

The Blade
The world runs on what we cast

The world is in an AI race, and the race has hit a ceiling. The ceiling is not chips or algorithms — it is energy. Energy is turbines. Turbines depend on one part above all others, the blade — cast in nickel super-alloy, grown as a single crystal, coated for temperatures hotter than the surface of the sun. There is essentially one company on Earth that makes it at scale. The bottleneck of the AI era is the blade. We are the blade.

Control-room screens show one territory's grid + alarms — not financial dashboards, not a global map. Map OK only as off-screen global context.

Data-center → software → insight is the spine. Robot arms, autonomous machines, "AI doing the work alone" are out. The data hallway shot is core.
